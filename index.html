A careful research companion.

What it does

SourceVault stores source text in this browser, finds matching passages, and helps you keep editable citation details beside your research. It does not browse the web, give legal advice, or verify Bluebook compliance.

How to use it

  1. Add a text-readable PDF, text file, or pasted source.
  2. Confirm the source information and add tags.
  3. Search your vault by keyword, phrase, concept, or subtopic.
  4. Review the returned passage and verify any draft citation before using it.

For this class prototype, use made-up or public materials only.
